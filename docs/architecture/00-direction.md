# 00 · Direction

Every view in this folder serves one direction. Read this first.

## One reputation for the internet, starting with Karwan's open market

Karwan's open market is where freelancers, people trading goods and services, and small businesses make agreements with each other, locally and across borders. Every agreement is protected by escrow, and every completed one becomes part of a record that travels with the person. Trade finance, purchase-order financing and invoice factoring, sits on the same escrow, so a good record turns into faster, cheaper money.

**What it is for:** internet trade that is simpler, faster and more reliable, with one reputation that goes wherever the person trades next.

**Where:** Nigeria first, then the regions behind Karwan's five languages: Africa, the Middle East and South Asia. Karwan does not claim to operate everywhere.

**How it feels:** like a person's normal banking app. They see a balance, a deal, a record and the next step. Chains, wallets and contracts work under the hood; once local currency is supported, crypto terms appear only behind "Proof".

**How people use it:** through their agent, in Telegram, on the website and in later channels. The same assistant does the same things everywhere. People do not need to see agent screens; they see Karwan deliver.

**The test for any piece of work:** does it make a trade in the open market simpler, faster or more reliable, or does it make the reputation from those trades more useful? If neither, it waits.

## How work is chosen

1. Every piece of work names its track and the roadmap item it serves before its view is drawn.
2. A track works on one item until a person can do what the item promised, verified on the stated network.
3. At most two tracks are active at once.
4. Events, grants and demo days can move an item earlier. They do not add new ones.
5. Work that serves no track waits in the parked list until it earns a place on a roadmap.

## What waits for now

Partner and developer pages wait for unified reputation (T3). The newsletter waits until the product works end to end. Separate freelancer contracts and the ProofStream idea are folded into the contract family: every deal asks which kind of agreement it is, and the matching contract carries it.
