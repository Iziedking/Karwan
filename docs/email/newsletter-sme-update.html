One Karwan account for personal and business trade

Karwan gives people and small businesses one way to move from a request or offer to a protected trade. Money sits in milestone escrow on Arc while the goods or service are delivered, then the buyer releases it against the agreed terms.

The current build keeps one person identity and login, with a personal workspace and an optional owner-only business workspace underneath. Businesses can switch context, publish what they offer, find supply, bring a deal, and keep one customer wallet and USDC balance. Business verification is separate from personal identity verification. Team permissions and financing remain planned work.

It is on Arc Testnet today. Testnet USDC has no real value. Open Karwan to explore the workspace flow and run a protected trade with test funds.
