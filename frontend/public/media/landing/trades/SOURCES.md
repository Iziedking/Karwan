# Clips behind the kinds of trade

All six come from Pexels, under the Pexels licence (https://www.pexels.com/license/). It allows free use, including in ads, with no credit needed, and lets us edit the clips. It does not allow showing the people in a bad light, or implying that they or any brand endorse Karwan.

We use these clips to show kinds of trade, never as customers. A tile names a kind of trade and an example amount, never a person, a quote or a claim that someone uses Karwan. Each one is replaced with real footage once a trader agrees to share their story.

How they were made (4 Nov 2026):
- Each clip is a 7 second cut from the middle of the 1080p source. Its last 0.6 seconds fade into its first, so the loop has no jump.
- The sound is removed and the video is re-encoded to H.264 at 25 fps:
  - `-1080.mp4`: at most 1.6 MB;
  - `-720.mp4`: at most 700 KB;
  - `-poster.webp`: the first frame, at most 120 KB.
